# Travel Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Standard reimbursement rule
Employees are reimbursed for reasonable travel expenses incurred on company business, including economy airfare or train fare, local transport, accommodation within the published city limits, and meals within the daily allowance. Claims must be submitted with receipts within 30 days of returning from the trip.

## 2. Approvals
All business trips require prior approval from the employee's line manager. Claims above the trip budget require additional approval from the department head.

## 3. International travel
For international trips, all receipts issued in a foreign currency must be submitted for foreign currency receipt verification by the Finance team. This verification is mandatory before any international claim is paid. The exchange rate applied is the rate on the date of the expense.

## 4. Late-booking exception
Travel booked after the travel date, for example a ticket purchased retrospectively for an emergency trip, is an exception to the standard rule. Such claims require approval from a senior director in addition to the normal approvals, and must include a written reason for the late booking.
