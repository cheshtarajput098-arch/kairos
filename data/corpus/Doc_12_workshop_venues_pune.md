# Workshop Venues in Pune

## 1. Overview
Veloria Systems maintains two approved venues in Pune for customer workshops, training days and partner meetings. Both venues are booked through the Events Desk. Teams must not book unapproved venues directly with a supplier.

## 2. Capacity and rooms
Venue A, Riverside Hall in Baner, seats up to 40 people in a classroom layout or 60 people theatre style. It has a built-in projector, two wireless microphones and a separate breakout room for 10 people.
Venue B, Koregaon Studio in Koregaon Park, seats up to 35 people in a classroom layout. It has a large display screen, a whiteboard wall and natural daylight.
For groups of more than 40 people, the Events Desk arranges an external venue on request.

## 3. Booking process
Submit a booking request on the Events Desk portal at least 10 working days before the event. Include the expected headcount, date, layout and any accessibility needs. The Events Desk confirms availability within 2 working days.

## 4. Accessibility
Both venues have step-free access and an accessible restroom. Riverside Hall has a hearing loop in the main room. Koregaon Studio has a lift to the first-floor workshop space.
