# Meeting Rooms in Bengaluru Office

## 1. Room types
The Bengaluru office has focus rooms for up to 4 people, team rooms for up to 12 people, and one boardroom for up to 20 people.

## 2. Booking rules
Rooms are booked through the office calendar. Bookings longer than 4 hours need approval from the office manager. Unused bookings are released automatically 15 minutes after the start time.

## 3. Equipment
Every team room has a video conferencing kit. The boardroom also has a document camera and a ceiling microphone array.
