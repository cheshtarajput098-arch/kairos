# IT Equipment Loans

## 1. Available equipment
Employees may borrow laptops, portable projectors, conference speakers and presentation clickers from the IT service desk.

## 2. Loan period
Standard loans last up to 5 working days. Extensions require a request to the IT service desk before the loan ends.

## 3. Responsibility
The borrower is responsible for returning equipment in working condition. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported within 24 hours.
