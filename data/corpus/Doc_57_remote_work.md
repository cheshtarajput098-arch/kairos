# Remote Work Policy

## 1. Eligibility
Employees who have completed their probation period may work remotely up to 2 days per week with their manager's agreement.

## 2. Equipment and security
Remote work must use company-issued devices connected through the company VPN. Confidential documents must not be printed at home.

## 3. Availability
Remote workers must be reachable during core hours, 11:00 to 16:00 local time.
