# Expense Receipt Standards

## 1. What counts as a valid receipt
A valid receipt shows the supplier name, date, amount, currency and a description of the goods or services. Card statements alone are not accepted as receipts.

## 2. Digital receipts
Photographs or scans of receipts are accepted if all details are legible. Original paper receipts must be kept for 90 days after the claim is paid.

## 3. Missing receipts
If a receipt is lost, the employee must submit a missing-receipt declaration signed by their line manager. Missing-receipt declarations are limited to two per calendar year.
