# Catering Options for Events

## 1. On-site catering
Koregaon Studio offers in-house catering with vegetarian and non-vegetarian lunch menus, tea and coffee service, and snacks. Orders must be confirmed 5 working days before the event. In-house catering is charged per person.

## 2. External caterers
Teams may use caterers from the approved supplier list. External caterers must provide a food safety certificate to the Events Desk before the event. The team organising the event is responsible for the caterer's deposit.

## 3. Dietary requirements
Organisers must collect dietary requirements from attendees at least 7 days in advance. Vegan, Jain and gluten-free options are available from all approved caterers when requested in advance.
