# Help us test a voice assistant (15 minutes)

Thank you for helping! We're building an assistant that answers questions from a set of company documents **while you're still speaking**. We need test questions written by people who haven't seen how it works.

## What we'd like from you
Write **8 short things a person might say** to this assistant, one of each type below, plus 3 of your choice. Write them the way people actually talk, including "uh", changing your mind mid-sentence, or adding details at the end.

**Topics the documents cover:**
- Travel reimbursement policy & submission deadlines (Doc_05)
- Workshop venues in Pune: capacities and AV facilities (Doc_12)
- Bengaluru office meeting rooms and booking rules (Doc_20)
- Event cancellation terms and refund notice periods (Doc_31)
- IT equipment loans (laptops, monitors) and loan durations (Doc_44)
- Remote work eligibility and core hours (Doc_57)
- Expense receipt standards and missing receipt affidavits (Doc_63)
- Catering options for events and dietary requirements (Doc_89)

1. **Compound:** one sentence asking two or three things at once. *e.g. "I need a room for 20 people next week and uh what's the refund if we cancel?"*
2. **Late detail:** a follow-up that changes something about your previous question. *e.g. "Actually, make that 45 people."*
3. **Reformat request:** asking to change how the last answer looks. *e.g. "Can you shorten that?"*
4. **Single question:** one simple question.
5. **Outside the documents:** something the documents probably don't cover. *e.g. "What's the weather in Pune tomorrow?"*

## Please don't
- Look up the documents' exact wording (we want natural phrasing).
- Share these questions with other helpers.

## How to send them
Paste them into the form or message we sent you, one per line, with the type number in front, for example `1: …`.
